An Evening of Splendor, Recognition & High Entertainment

Join over 400 leaders, visionaries, and honored guests for our prestigious Annual Gala & Success Celebration. Hosted within the historic Grand Palace Ballroom in Manhattan, this black-tie evening commences with a rooftop vintage champagne reception followed by a 4-course banquet prepared by award-winning guest chefs.

The night features live swing and classical orchestra performances, our prestigious innovation awards ceremony, and an exclusive midnight after-party with renowned international DJs.
