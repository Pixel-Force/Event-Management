30-Year Fixed Rate Mortgage

Home / Loan Programs / 30-Year Fixed
Conventional Financing

The Gold Standard of Long-Term Home Financing

The 30-year fixed rate mortgage is America's most popular home financing vehicle, accounting for over 70% of all purchase loans. It provides complete predictability: your interest rate and monthly principal-and-interest payment remain constant throughout the entire 360-month term, shielding you from broader macroeconomic interest rate volatility.

Key Program Advantages

Unwavering Payment Stability

Your principal and interest payment will never change, regardless of how high inflation or Federal Reserve rates climb.

Lower Monthly Payment Burden

Spreading your loan balance across 30 years yields the lowest monthly payment compared to 15-year or 20-year alternatives.

Automatic PMI Removal

Unlike FHA loans where mortgage insurance can last for the life of the loan, conventional PMI automatically cancels once you hit 20% equity.

Eligibility Requirements Checklist

  • Minimum Credit Score: 620 for standard conventional financing (680+ for premier tier pricing).
  • Minimum Down Payment: 3% for qualified first-time homebuyers; 5% for repeat buyers.
  • Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio: Maximum 45% (up to 50% with strong compensating factors and automated underwriter approval).
  • Loan Limit: Up to $766,550 for single-family residences (higher in designated high-cost counties).
  • Property Eligibility: Single-family homes, townhouses, planned unit developments (PUDs), and Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac warrantable condominiums.
