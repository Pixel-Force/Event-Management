General Event Planning

How early should we start the planning process?

For major weddings and large-scale corporate summits (over 200 guests), we recommend starting 9 to 12 months in advance. For private milestone birthdays and intimate dinners, 6 to 12 weeks is typically sufficient.

What is the difference between Day-Of Coordination and Full Production?

Day-Of Coordination steps in 4 to 6 weeks before your date to take over vendor communication and execute your existing timeline. Full Production begins at concept phase: budgeting, venue sourcing, aesthetic design, vendor contracting, and total stage management.

Can we customize any of your standard packages?

Absolutely! Every couple and company has distinctive priorities. We will build an itemized proposal around your exact requirements, omitting elements you do not need.

Venues, Catering & Logistics

Do you assist with venue contracts and licensing?

Yes. We review venue insurance requirements, noise curfew ordinances, load-in protocols, and beverage liability agreements on your behalf to avoid surprise surcharges.

How do you handle dietary preferences or international cuisines?

Our culinary partners cater to all diets: vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher, and regional fusion. We arrange private tasting sessions where you sample the menu prior to finalizing.

Our lead coordinators are on standby to discuss your date, venue, or budget.
