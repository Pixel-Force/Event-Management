Mortgage Calculation Questions

Key financial terms explained to help you maximize your savings.

How is monthly principal and interest calculated?

We use the standard institutional amortization formula: \(M = P \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}\) where \(P\) is the principal loan amount, \(r\) is the monthly interest rate, and \(n\) is the total number of monthly payments.

What triggers Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)?

On conventional loans, putting down less than 20% requires PMI to protect the lender against default. Once your loan balance reaches 80% of your property's value, PMI is automatically cancelled.
