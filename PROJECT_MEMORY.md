## 2. Core Architecture & Features Built
- **Zero WordPress Footprint:** 100% bespoke semantic HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript without any WordPress generator tags, classes, or PHP dependencies.
- **14 Production-Ready HTML5 Pages:**
  1. `index.html` — Homepage 1 (Flagship Mortgage Broker: Hero with Quick Quote Widget, Live Rates Ticker, Core Services, Why Choose Us, Interactive Mortgage Calculator with Live Amortization, 4-Step Process, Rate Comparison Matrix, Loan Officers Team, Testimonials, Market News, Consultation Modal)
  2. `home-two.html` — Homepage 2 (Fintech & Digital Lending Edition with Instant Wholesale Quote Finder)
  3. `about.html` — About Us (Company Background, Leadership, Mission, Values, Milestones)
  4. `loan-programs.html` — Filterable Directory of all Loan Programs (Purchase, Refinance, FHA, VA, Jumbo, Commercial, Reverse Mortgages)
  5. `loan-details.html` — Program Deep Dive (Eligibility checklist, required documents, terms)
  6. `calculator.html` — Advanced Interactive Mortgage & Full Amortization Suite (Monthly P&I, Property Taxes, Insurance, PMI, dynamic SVG Donut Chart, and exportable/printable annual & monthly amortization schedules)
  7. `apply.html` — 4-Step Interactive Pre-Approval Application Wizard with Instant Pre-Approval Letter Generation
  8. `team.html` — Licensed Loan Officers with NMLS identifiers and strategy consultation booking modal
  9. `faqs.html` — Comprehensive Categorized Knowledge Base with search filtering
  10. `blog.html` — Market Insights & Homebuyer Guides
  11. `blog-details.html` — Article View with typography, author biography, and key takeaways
  12. `contact.html` — Branch office directory, contact details, and interactive lead inquiry form
  13. `privacy-policy.html` — Consumer Financial Privacy Policy (GLBA compliant)
  14. `terms-and-conditions.html` — Equal Housing Opportunity & Lending Disclosures
- **Interactive Functional Features:**
  - Real-time precision mortgage calculator with synchronized sliders, dual down payment inputs ($ and %), PMI logic, dynamic SVG Donut Chart visualization, and full amortization schedule table (Annual and Monthly toggles).
  - 4-Step Interactive Loan Application & Pre-Approval Wizard generating simulated conditional pre-approval letters.
  - Live animated benchmark mortgage rates ticker.
  - Interactive consultation booking modal, site search modal, and toast alerts.
  - Mobile off-canvas drawer navigation with submenus.
  - Full accreditation assets (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, VA, BBB, NMLS, Equal Housing Opportunity).
