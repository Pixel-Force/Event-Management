Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book your wedding planning team?

We recommend booking 8 to 14 months before your intended wedding date to secure your desired venue and elite vendors. However, our rapid response team can also execute bespoke weddings within 90 days.

Do you work with our own preferred vendors or only your directory?

We are entirely happy to collaborate with any licensed and insured vendors you have selected! We also maintain a strictly curated, trusted network of florists, caterers, and photographers.
