Conventional financing guidelines set a 620 baseline credit score. However, borrowers with credit scores of 740 and above unlock premier wholesale tier pricing and the lowest private mortgage insurance rates. For scores between 580 and 619, government-backed FHA loans offer an ideal low-rate alternative.

Your break-even point is calculated by dividing your total closing costs by your monthly savings. For example, if refinancing costs $3,000 and saves you $250 per month, your break-even period is 12 months. If you plan to remain in the home longer than 12 months, refinancing produces substantial net savings.
A cash-out refinance replaces your existing mortgage with a new, larger loan balance, allowing you to withdraw the difference in tax-free cash at closing. You can typically borrow up to 80% of your home's current appraised market value.
Closing costs encompass title insurance, escrow charges, county recording fees, appraisal fees, and prepaid property taxes and insurance. They generally range between 2% and 4% of the purchase price. We provide lender credit programs that can offset these costs entirely.
Do NOT open new credit cards, finance a vehicle, make large unexplained cash deposits into your bank accounts, or change employers during escrow without speaking with your loan officer first. Any significant changes can alter your debt-to-income ratio and jeopardize approval.
