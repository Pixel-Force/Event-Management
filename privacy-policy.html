Client Confidentiality & Data Protection

At Event Management, client discretion, privacy, and security are fundamental tenets of our service. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information submitted through our ticket reservations and consultation forms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect contact details (names, email addresses, phone numbers) and event specifications (dates, guest counts, budget guidelines) exclusively to provide event coordination quotes and process ticket reservations.

2. Non-Disclosure & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade client information. For high-profile VIP weddings, private milestone galas, and corporate product unveilings, all production staff and sub-contractors execute strict confidentiality non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

3. Contact

For questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us at privacy@eventmanagement.com.
