Consumer Privacy Notice & Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 01, 2026

Mortgage Broker LLC ("Mortgage Broker", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of non-public personal information (NPI) collected from prospective and active borrowers. This privacy notice explains how we collect, safeguard, and use your financial data under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) and applicable federal and state lending laws.

1. Information We Collect

In evaluating loan applications and determining eligibility for mortgage programs, we collect information provided directly by you, including:

  • Identifying details (Full name, address, Social Security Number, date of birth)
  • Financial assets and liabilities (Income, bank accounts, outstanding debts, tax returns, W-2s)
  • Credit history and credit scores retrieved from consumer reporting agencies
  • Property details relating to the real estate being purchased or refinanced

2. How We Use and Share Information

We do NOT sell your personal financial information to non-affiliated third parties for marketing. We share borrower information strictly to facilitate loan origination and underwriting with:

  • Wholesale lending partners and secondary market investors evaluating your loan scenario
  • Licensed title companies, escrow officers, property appraisers, and flood certification providers
  • Credit reporting bureaus and automated verification platforms (e.g., Fannie Mae Desktop Underwriter)
  • State and federal regulatory bodies as required by statutory compliance audits

3. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards that comply with federal regulations to protect your non-public personal information. All digital data transfers, document uploads, and application forms are secured using modern TLS 1.3 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. Contact Regarding Privacy Rights

If you have questions about your privacy rights or wish to exercise state consumer data rights, contact our Privacy Compliance Officer at privacy@mortgagebroker.com or call (800) 555-2345.
